The role

I joined Frido's Founder's Office as an Assistant Manager and was promoted to AVP within two years. The Founder's Office is the job with no fixed edges — context-switching, problem-solving and ownership, usually all three before lunch.

The best insights don't come from textbooks. They come from the ground. I've spent that time on the floor: with the sales team, inside the retail operation, in the review calls where the numbers stop being a slide and start being a decision.

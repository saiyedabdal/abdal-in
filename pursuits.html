Pursuits

The things I chased outside the job

Rooms I talked my way into, competitions I entered, and work that had nothing to do with a salary. Some of it went well. All of it taught me something the job could not.

3International bodies
2National podium
1Represented India

01

Development & diplomacy

Work with UNDP Asia-Pacific, the Office of the Vice President of the Philippines, and the Asian Development Bank in Manila — a stretch that put me in rooms where policy and delivery meet, a long way from where I started.

02

Competing

National second runner-up at the Smart India Hackathon — the country's largest hackathon, and the first time engineering felt like a contact sport rather than a syllabus.

Represented India at a public speaking championship in London. Toastmasters taught me the craft; that stage taught me what it holds up under.

03

Building on my own

Scaled a healthcare corporation from zero to one across operations and business development — the version of zero-to-one where there is no playbook and no team yet.

Crossed ₹1 crore in revenue as an independent distributor. Built without a brand behind me, which is the only way to learn what selling actually is.

04

On film

The application film I made for the Global Changemakers Global Youth Summit — two minutes to say who I am and why it should be me.

Global Changemakers · Global Youth Summit application

05

Speaking

A Toastmaster, and the kind who kept going after the certificate. Public speaking is the through-line across most of this — the hackathon pitch, the summit application, the investor deck, the poetry read aloud.

    • Concept of Diffusion

      Diffusion is movement of particle from High Concentration of Salt to Low Concentration

      For Example :

      1. when we mix salt and water

      2. Exchange of O2 and CO2 in Lungs in Blood is due to Diffusion

        Breathe In - Oxygen && Hamari Nas Bring in De Oxygenated Blood from the Body

    • Concept of Osmosis

      1. It is a Special Case of Diffusion only in case of Liquids
      2. Hence Diffusion of Liquid is Osmosis
      3. Diffusion is a Generic Term and Osmosis is a Specific Terms
      4. In Osmosis, Water from Higher Concentration moves to Water from Lower Concentration
      5. Here Having a Membrane is a must
      6. Example:
        1. Bloating in fingers when bathes for too much time or when immersed in water
        2. Bloating of Raisins when immersed in water
        3. Movement of Water from Soil to Roots of Plants

    • Concept of Osmoregulation

      1. Osmoregulation is the process by which organisms regulate water or electrolyte level in the body.

      2. Electrolytic Balance in its body to maintain Homeostasis Balance

      3. Osmoregulation in Animals is carried out by well developed excretory system

        In Human Kidney is the Principal Organ to Maintain Water and Electrolyte Balance in the Body

      4. Osmoregulation in Plants is carried out by Vacuoles

        1. Dry / Arid Environment →Store More Water in Vacuoles
        2. Wet Environment→ More Transpiration

    • Concept of Transpiration

      1. Transpiration of Evaporation of Water from Leaves. Some times also called Evapotranspiration
      2. the process of water passing out from the surface of a plant or leaf

    • Concept of Homeostasis

      1. the process by which the body reacts to changes in order to keep conditions inside the body, for example temperature; the same
      2. Homeostasis is the response to keep the condition inside the body same
      3. Homeostasis Balance is a Biological Equilibrium State
      4. Examples
        1. When we feel heat we perspire
        2. In Winter We Shiver due to Contraction of muscles for maintaining heat

      Cell is Fundamental Unit of Life. The Term was coined by Robert Hook in 1665. Human Body is composed of Trillions of Cells. They provide Structure to Body, take in Nutrients from food and converts nutrients into energy

        2. Outside Nucleus - Cytoplasm

    1. Longest Bone in Human Body - Femur

    2. Shortest Bone in Human Body - Stapes (Ear Bone)

    3. Child Has → 300+ Bones

    4. Human Has → 206 Bones

    5. Total No of Vertebra → 33

    6. No of Ribs → 12 on Each Side 24 in Total

    7. Cranial Capacity → 1400 to 1500 cc (1 Cubic Centimetere = 1.5 Ltr Water)

    8. No of Bones in Skull → 29 Bones

    9. Allen & Bergman Rule →

      Organisms Aapt to Environment in Colder Regions to reduce the Surface Area.

      The Limbs and Extremities that is the Fingers become shorter in Length and the body becomes bulky or stalkier

      Example: Eskimos & Innuits, Seal & Polar Bear → Genetic Adaptations

      Organism Living in Hotter Region, Organisms need to Dissipate Heat, so they have longer limbs and extremities and Lean body.

      Examples: Tribes of Africa

    10. Gloggers Rule

      In Hotter Region, the Melanin Content Increases to protect against UV Radiation.

      In Colder Region the Melanin Content Reduces.

    • Blood & its Details

      • Basic Facts
        1. Blood is the Only Mobile Connective Tissue
        2. There is 5 to 6 Litres of Blood in Human Body
        3. Blood is Slightly Basic in Nature i.e its pH is 7.4
      • Parts of Blood are

        • Plasma - 60%

          After Anti Body is made in WBC. Anti Body is present in Plasma.

          Plasma has proteins which contains Anti Bodies.

          Removing Proteins from the Plasma we get Serum

        • Corpuscles - 40%

          • RBC’s → Erythrocytes

            1. It is Bi Concave in Shape
            2. Helps in carrying Oxygen. It carries a pigment containing Iron called as Haemoglobin
            3. Mature RBC cells do not have a Nucleus
            4. RBC’s are formed in Bone Marrow
            5. in Bone Marrow there is Blood Stem Cells which helps in formation of Blood
            6. Life of RBC is just for few months
            7. Problems with RBC

              1. Anaemia

                Def of Iron → Def of Haemoglobin → We Need 12 to 16 gms / dl of Haemoglobin in the Body → If this is reduced then oxygen is not supplied properly

              2. Sickle Cell

                RBC becomes shape of a Sickle Cell

              3. Thalassaemia

                insufficient qty of Hb. Here Bone Marrow is not able to make sufficient qty of RBC. Here Transfusion of Blood (i.e giving blood externally) is done as a cure. Stem cell therapy is a relatively newer

              4. Leukemia

                It is blood cancer. Here formation of RBC & WBC may be hampered

              5. Haemophilia (also called as Royal Disease)

                Here Blood is not able to clot. Here men are more affected than women.

          • WBC’s → Leucocytes

            T Cells (Killer Cells) B Cells (Help in Making Anti Bodies) Eosinophils, Basophils, Neutrophils

          • Platelets → Thrombocytes

            Plasma is necessary to help your body recover from injury, distribute nutrients, remove waste and prevent infection, while moving throughout your circulatory system

    • Concepts Related to Inheritance of Blood Group

      Concept of Mendel Law, Genotype, Phenotype

      Genotype - The Genes which an Individual has

      Phenotype - Physical manifestation of the Gene

      Example: Genotype - XX Phenotype - Female

      Example: Genotype - XY Phenotype - Male

      %20(1)/Screenshot_2023-03-08_at_2.21.35_PM.png)

      If the Blood Group is B then the Geno Type is AA or AO

      If the Blood Group is A then Geno Type is BB or BO

      If the Blood Group is O then Geno Type is OO only

      Alleles are the different forms of the same trait

      in AO → A is a Allele & O is Allele

      Example Height is one Characteristics but T is allele for Tall and t is allele for Short

      Similarly → in Tt Gene → T is tall height Allele & t is small height Allele

      Concept of Mendel Law by Gregor Mendel, 1865

      He is known as Father of Genetics. He gave two Laws.

      Law of Segregation

      Alleles Segregate during Gamete Formation.

      Alleles can be Dominant or Recessive

      Dominant Alleles are Allele which Express themselves which are expresses even Singly in the Genes

      Recessive Allele doesn’t express itself if it is present singly. here tt will express itself

      Application of this Concept to Inheritance of Blood

      in Blood Group → A & B are Dominant Alleles && O is recessive allele

      AO & AA → A is Expressed as Phenotype

      BB & BO → B is Expressed as Phenotype

      OO → O is Expressed as Phenotype

    • Blood Circulation System

      %20(1)/Screenshot_2023-03-08_at_2.44.46_PM.png)

      Blood Vessel is a Generic Term → They can be called as Arteries, Vein, Capillary

      Arteries → Which Carries blood Away from Heart to Different Parts of Body. Hence, Carries Oxygen Rich blood. Blood Flows at Higher Pressure in Arteries. Arteries are more thicker than Veins.

      Veins → They carry De Oxygenated Blood from different parts of Body towards heart. Here Pressure of the Blood in the Veins is Less. Veins are less thick as compared to Arteries

      Capillary → It is thinnest blood vessel which is just one cell thick. It helps in Diffusion of Gases.

      Impure Blood → Impure Blood is De Oxygenated Blood

      Working of Heart→ When Left Ventricle Compresses Oxygenated Blood is pumped into the Arteries. It reaches the Capillaries where Oxygen from Blood Diffuses to that part of Body and Carbon Di Oxide diffuses into RBC.

      Now, this De Oxygenated Blood moves in the Veins to reach heart. The Rt Ventricle pumps this De Oxygenated Blood into Lungs, where Oxygen Diffuses Into Blood and CO2 Diffuses out of Blood and this Oxygenated Blood reaches Left Autricle and then the Left Ventricle again compresses

    • Diseases Related to Circulatory System

      1. Concept of Systolic & Diastolic

        Blood Pressure is Measured using Sphygmanometere. it measures the pressure of Arteries only

        Systolic → when it is in contraction state or pumping state

        Diastolic → when it is in relaxation state

        In a Normal Person Range is 120/80 → Systolic & Diastolic

      2. Concept of High BP & Low BP

        Hypertension → Range is > 140/90.

      3. CAD → Coronary Artery Disease

        Coronary Arteries are the Blood Vessels which Supply Blood to Heart Muscles, if this supply is blocked because of Deposition of Fat or Calcium. Then it can lead to Heart Attack

        Stents are Metallic Tube Like Structures which are placed inside Blood Vessels so that the Blood flow is not restricted.

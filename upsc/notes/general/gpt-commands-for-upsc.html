  • for Current Affairs Notes

    Transform the provided content into structured and concise revision notes, specifically tailored for current affairs topics relevant to UPSC prelims and mains exams. The notes should be organized hierarchically, capturing key points, essential definitions, and significant developments. Include any relevant examples, case studies, or policy implications. Ensure that the notes are comprehensive yet succinct, covering all crucial aspects of the current affairs material. Use bullet points, numbering, and subheadings to enhance clarity and facilitate easy recall. The aim is to distill the content into an easily reviewable format, aiding in effective preparation and revision for UPSC examination candidates

  • for Making Notes out of Transcript

    • Prompt Part 1

      "Please convert the provided transcript into detailed, explained, and lucid class notes using the following guidelines:

      1. Structure: Organize the content with a nested numbered bullet system for clarity and easy reference.
      2. Content Flow: Ensure the information flows logically, making it easy to comprehend.
      3. Detail & Lucidity: Provide a thorough and clear explanation of each point, ensuring every concept is easily understandable. Do not provide a brief summary; instead, retain the depth and richness of the original content.
      4. Emphasis on Highlights: Any content that is highlighted or underlined in the transcript must be prominently retained and emphasized in the notes.
      5. Redundancy: Eliminate repetitive information, but ensure all essential details are retained.
      6. Completeness: Retain all crucial facts, words, or concepts from the original transcript.
      7. Hierarchy: Organize the information hierarchically, with main ideas at the forefront and supporting details following.

      Important: In the process of organizing or correcting the flow, do not remove or alter any facts, words, or specific details provided in the original sentences. Retaining the integrity of the provided information is crucial.

      Note to ChatGPT: It's imperative that the notes derived from the transcript are extremely detailed. The essence, depth, and richness of the original content should be fully captured in the notes, ensuring no loss of information."

      Note to ChatGPT: If I have put something in between this , then no matter what it has to be made a heading and date below that has to be included in the notes

    • Prompt Part 2

      Tables:

      • Timeline Table: Include a table listing all dates and years from the transcript.
      • Concepts Table: Compile a table of all concepts from the transcript, summarizing each with its definition.
      • Personalities Table: Detail all personalities mentioned in the transcript in a separate table.

    • Prompt Part 3

      Note to ChatGPT : If i have said somewhere in the notes, that this has to be put in quotes or this should be made list of then please make a separate list of all these lines in the last after the tables

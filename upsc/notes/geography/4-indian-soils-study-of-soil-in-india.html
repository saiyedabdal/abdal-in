  • Factors Affecting Soil Formation

    • Parent Material

      Soil can be - In Situ or Ex Situ (Indo Ganegetic)

      Humus Gives → Dark Colors to Soil

    • Climate

      • Process of Leaching for Tropical and Equatorial Climate

        Tropical Climates & Equatorial Climates are → Hot & Humid

        In Layers of Soil there is Al, Fe, Si → Here Temp and Rainfall is very high

        There can be dry to wet spells.

        In this process Silica will get mixed with water and move downards

        Here upper layer is left with Oxides of Iron and Alluminium as Silica has gone Down

        Sun will act on this Oxides, it will break this Iron and the Soil will be called as Laterite Soil or Red Soil

        This Process is called as Laterisation

        Here if Al > Fe → Alfisols

        Here if Fe > Al → Ferrosols

      • Process of Podsolisation for Temperate Climate of Himalayas in Tropical Areas

        1. Rainfall and Evaporation are allmost Equal
        2. Here Soil Moisture will be Balanced too
        3. Coniferous Evergreen Vegetation Leaves have Wax Coating, Hence Relative Moisture Content is High and Transpiration is Low
        4. In Process of Decomposition these Leaves will fall on ground and release water
        5. This Iron Oxides Reacting with water from Leaves and taking air from atm
        6. Here Al and Fe will go down the layers of Soil and Upper Layer will be left with Silica only

      • Process of Salinisation of Deserts

        1. In Desets Evaporation > Precipitation
        2. Hence, Salt will be pumped to the Surface
        3. this is called Salinisation of Soil

      • Difference Between Leaching and Cheluviation

        1. In Leaching Water in action is of Rain Water or River Water
        2. In Cheluviation Water in action is from Chluviating Agents like Leaf etc

  • Processes Involved in Soil Formation

    • Organic Accumulation*

      This Process Involves the Accumulation of Decaying Vegetables and Animal Matter on the Ground Surface, the Accumulated Mass Slowly Breaks down to form Humus which is a Major Component of the Soil

      Humus Detemines Fertility of the Soil

    • Elluviation*

      This is the Mechanical Washdown of Finer Mineral Particles from the Upper Layer of the Soil into the Middle Lower Layer of the Soul

      Elluviation can also take place in the same layer when the water percolates horizontally

      The Upper Layer from which material has been elluviated is called as Elluvial Zone

      Process - Elluviation

      Jahan Deposition Hua - Illuviation

      Jahan se Deposite Hone ke Liye Nikla - Elluviation Layer

    • Leaching*

      This is the Removal of Soluble Mineral Material in the Solution from the Upper Layer (Horizon) to the Lower Horizon of the Soil. The Process can be referred as Chemical Elluviation

      It is common in Wet Climates

      Leaching of Minerals causes the Top Soil to be Deficient of Minerals.

    • Illuviation

      Illuviation is the Deposition of Material that has been washed down from upper layer to the lower horizon

    • Precipitation

      This is the Formation of Solid Material in the Sub Soil. The Solid material is formed from the Solution washed down from Upper Layers through Leaching, Elluviation, Illuviation and precipitation takes place at the same time under the same climatic condition

    • Cheluviation*

      This is the Downward Movement of Material in the Soil which is Similar to Leaching. However, Cheluviation occurs through the influence of Organic Agents also called Chelating Agents

      The Process Involves Plant Acids Rather than Mere Water as was in the Case of Leaching

    • Organic Sorting

      This is the Separation of material usually of Different Size through Organic Influence

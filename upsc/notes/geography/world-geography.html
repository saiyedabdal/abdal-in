        • Proof 1 - Jig Saw Matching of Continents

            3. Ex 3 - Horn of Africa - Western India & Pakistan
          2. Object : Attempt to Match Present Coastline which might have changed due to Waves & Tides in 100MY
          3. RePropose : Bullard, 1964 - Made Map of Deep Ocean Coastline
            1. To Rule out Effect of Waves & Tides as no tides in Deep Ocean

        • Proof 2 - Similar Rocks on both Sides of Ocean

          1. Coastal Deposits(Rocks) From - Brazilian Coast && Western African Coast

            • Weathering
              • Intro to Weathering
                1. Due to Atmospheric Pressure, Moisture, Ecological Agents ; Makes Rock Weak → Eventually Break
                2. Causes Physical & Chemical Changes
                3. Occurs at IN-Situ
                4. No large scale transportation of weathered material except for Mass Movement
              • Types of Weathering
                • Physical
                  • Intro
                    1. Disintegration & Reduction in Size of Rock w/o Change in Chemical Composition
                    2. In Dry & Cold Regions
                  • Types
                    • Exfoliation
                      1. Due to Vertical Pressure Release ; Layer by Layer Peeling
                      2. Primarily in Intrusive Igneous & Metamorphosed Rocks
                      3. Can Occur Slowly & Rapidly
                      4. Forms Crack Parallel to Curved Landforms → Exfoliation Slabs → Exfoliation Domes
                    • Frost Wedging / Freeze Thaw
                      1. Caused by Freeze Thaw Action of Water
                      2. Water Freezes (Anomalous Behaviour) → Expands Applies Pressure to Accommodate Expansion of Ice
                      3. Generally Produces Angular Blocks
                    • Salt Wedging
                      1. Salt in Stone Crystallises as Solution with Evaporation of Water
                      2. As Salt Crystal Grows → They Apply Pressure to Surrounding Rock → Weakening & Breaking
                      3. In Drier Climate - Deserts
                    • Temperature Change
                      1. Due to Diurnal (DailY) & Seasonal Temp Changes
                      2. Causes Expansion & Contraction of Rocks Faults & Surfaces
                      3. Dominant in Desert Climates
                      4. Granite - Smooth Surface - Rounded Small to Big Boulder - Tors
                      5. Not Main Reason for Weathering but accelerates other forms
                    • Abrasion
                      1. When Rocks Collide Against Each Other
                      2. Constant Collision or Fall due to Gravity - Causes Weakening & Breaking
                      3. Flowing Water - Primary Medium of Abrasion - Makes Fluvial Sediments
                      4. During Abrasion - Bed Rock Surfaces also weathered
                • Chemical
                  • Intro
                    1. Dissolve, Alters & Weaken rocks through Chemical Processes
                    2. in Humid & Tropical Climatic Region
                  • Types
                    • Carbonation
                      1. CO2+ Rainwater - Weak Acid - Carbonic Acid → Carbonate Rocks (CaCo3) → Calcium Bi-Carbonate (Ca(HCO3)2)
                      2. Easily Dissolved in Water Now - Weakening & Breaking
                      3. IN wet & Moist Climate
                      4. Lime Stone & Dolomite & Fine Clayey Particles
                    • Oxidation
                      1. O2+H20 → Iron Rich Minerals → Rusting → Red Pellets on Rocks
                      2. Weaken Structure → Changes Colour
                      3. Oxidation Accelerates Rock Decay
                    • Hydration
                      1. Addition of Water to Minerals
                      2. Mineral Absorb Water → Positive Change of Volume
                      3. New Compound Larger than Previous One → Expansion
                      4. Now this New Compound Formation → Stress & Strain in Rocks → Weakening & breaking of Rocks
                      5. Can Cause Colour Change as well ; Accompanies by Hydrolysis & Oxidation
                    • Hydrolysis
                      1. Reaction Between H+ & OH- Particles
                      2. H+ ions + Mineral → Weak Acid
                      3. Reaction creates new Compound → Soft & Weak
                      4. Hydrolysis → Mineral Expand → Causes Further Expansion of Rocks
                      5. Ex : Anhydrite (CaSo4)+2 Molecules = CaSO4.2H20)
                    • Solution
                      1. Minerals & Rocks Directly into water
                      2. Generally on Rocks Containing Carbonate → LimeStone
                      3. Affects Rock with Larger Amount of Halite → Rock Salt
                      4. Solution of Large Areas of BEDROCK → Cause SINKHOLE
                • Biological
                  1. Caused by Chemical and Physical Agents of Organisms
                  2. Types
                    • Plant (Floral)
                      • Bio Chemical - Plant Roots
                      • Bio Physical - Organic Material of Dead Flora & Fauna
                        1. Secretion of Organic Acids by Dead Flora & Fauna by - Humic Acid / Bacterial Acid
                        2. Produces Sulphides, Oxidise Iron, Help in Solution of Silica
                    • Animal (Faunal)
                      1. Burrowing, Tunneling
                      2. Acid Secretion - Biological Weathering + Mechanical Weathering
                      3. Mosses & Lichens
                      4. Small Animals, Worms, Termites & Other Insects
                    • Anthropogenic Weathering
                      1. Mining, Blasting of Hills, Dam Construction, Mineral Extraction
                      2. Weathering on Hill Slopes → Modified Ground Structure → Via Deforestation → Reduces (Mech Reinforcement + Cohesion of Unconsolidated Rocks) → Slope Instability → Slope Failures → Mass Movement
              • Differential Weathering
                1. Rate of Weathering Varies with Rock(Softness/Hardness), Texture & Other Characteristics & Varied Climatic Condition
                2. Contributes to Unique Land Forms Pedestals, Waterfalls, Monadnocks
                3. Ex : Limestone weathers Quickly in Wet Climate rather than in Dry Climate

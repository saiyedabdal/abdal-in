        1. Pacific Ocean - Largest in Size
        2. The Pacific Ocean is the largest and the deepest ocean on average
        3. It is also called as Pacific Ring of Fire
        4. It is twice the size of the Atlantic Ocean
        5. The Pacific Ocean has the deepest point in the ocean ie Marian Trench and Mariana Trench has Challenger Deep (10,900 Mts)

        7. Highest Point on Earth is Mt Everest 8848. Hence Highest point on Earth is less than Deepest point on Sea
        1. Atlantic Ocean - Second Largest in Size
        2. It is the youngest Ocean 250 million years is its age, becasue of Continental Drift and Sea Floor Spreading.
        3. It is the 2nd largest ocean and is in S-shape
        4. The most prominent feature is the Mid-Atlantic Ridge which is also in S-shape
        1. Indian Ocean - Third Largest in Size
        2. Only Ocean to be named after a country
        3. The Indian Ocean is connected to land towards the North
        4. Responsible for Monsoon and dictates weather of land north to Indian Ocean

        1. Arctic Ocean - Fourth Largest in Size

        2. Arctic Ocean - It is the smallest ocean out of all the 5 oceans

        3. It has no landmass and is covered with ice

        4. The area under the ice is decreasing due to Global warming

        5. The polar bear is the largest land carnivore which is there in the Arctic

        6. Due to Arctic amplification - the impact of Global warming is more as it has a fragile ecosystem

          the rate of increase of temperature is high here

        7. According to report of IPCC → Also weakening of Atlantic meridional overturning circulation (AMOC) is taking place → Reduced Intensity of Ocean Currents

        8. Human beings have been existing here for long times; Also

        9. Neanderthals also existed here

        1. Southern Ocean
        2. Southern Ocean is the fifth Ocean and is the recent addition i.e in the year 2000 by International Hydrographic Organisation (IHO)
        3. Rationale - As it does not mix with other waters easily due to westerlies
        4. Southern Ocean is until 60 degree south latitude
        5. Unofficially the Southern Ocean is also called Austral Ocean (Meaning of Australis is South) or Antarctica Ocean

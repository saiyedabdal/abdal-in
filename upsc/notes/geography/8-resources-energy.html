  • What is a Feedstock

    Feedstock is the Raw Material to Supply or Fuel a machine or Industrial Process

    Example :

    1. NEPTA Obtained from Crude Oil is used in Manufacturing of Fertilisers and Gasolines
    2. Corns and Paddies used to produce Ethanol
    3. Soyabean Oil and Corn used for Production of Bio Diesel
    4. Sweet Gas of CH4, NH3 used for Manufacturing of Urea

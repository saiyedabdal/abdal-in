  • Important Concepts for Case Studies to be understood beforehand

    • Ethical Dilemma

      1. Ethical Dilemma occurs when people have to choose between morally correct choices
      2. This paper will present Ethical Dilemmas to you, presented choices would not be unambiguously right or wrong.
      3. Sometimes there are No Right or Wrong Answers. There you have to choose a course of action and then justify it
      4. Hence Justification is more important than the choice itself. You have to justify your rationale
      5. Thar Rationales is only right which is pragmatic
      6. Examiner is more interested in why is it that you did something, rather than what u did
      7. Example of Ethical Dilemma : Ethical Dilemma by Mathuradas in the movie Border

    • Public Trust

      1. Public trust doctrine is a legal principle establishing that certain natural and cultural resources are preserved for public use.
      2. Public trust gives legitimacy to the government

    • Conflict of Interests (Real, Apparent & Potential)

      A conflict of interest occurs when an individual's personal interests – family, friendships, financial, or social factors – could compromise his or her judgment, decisions, or actions in the workplace

      Real - Exist Karta Hai Apparent - Exist karta hai lekin abhi tak samne nahin aaya hai Potential - May exist as visible to third party

      An actual/real conflict of interest involves a direct conflict between a public official's current duties and responsibilities and existing private interests.

      A potential conflict of interest arises where a public official has private interests that could conflict with their official duties in the future.

  • Writing Approach

    • Thinking Process

      Think about the stand you will take before starting to write the answers

    • Role Playing

      1. You should think and act like you have “Skin in the Game”.

      2. In any role playing the Doctrine of Public Trust should not be broken

      3. Be Aware of your rights and duties in that post. Hence, as a role player, awareness makes you logical

        Example : DM can only Impose Sec 144, Not the SP

    • Decision Making

      1. It is important for us to take decisons, hence it is important for us to practise case studies as a civil servant. Case Studies practise will help you travel a journey from being a Critique to a Decision Maker
      2. Make yourself a Decision Maker, You should translate yourself to the Case Study
      3. In Decision Making, you have to choose between various competing values
      4. Choices must be explainable
      5. Think About Constraints → Take the Right Decision → Be Real and Pragmatic
      6. It is Sahin v/s Sabke Liye Accha

    • Writing Style

      It should be like conversing with someone. in this case the examiner

    • Cohernce

      1. There should be a Flow of Answers in the thought
      2. Coherence of thoughts and ideology in every part of the answer should be seen
      3. Flow of Argument → There should be No Disconnect → i.e No Inconsistency in your answers

    • Conviction

      1. Answers should be written with conviction
      2. Aisa Nahi Lagna Chahiye ki Likh Raha Hai par karega kuch aur

    • Important Note

      1. Leave the Idea that all stakeholders will be Satisfied. Someone or the other would either be unhappy or be dissatisfied
      2. Once you identify the demerits, then you can work to address them either in the medium term or long term - This Part will get you marks

  • Structure of a Case Studies Answer - Detailed

    • Introduction :

      1. Give Insights to Examiner about what is to come
      2. Tell the Examiner in Introduction that why do you think this question has been asked or what is the inspiration behind this question
      3. For Examples
        1. In Light of some constitutional value
        2. or some feature of our economy

    • Step 1 - Identify Facts of the Case

      Identify the facts of the Case

    • Step 2 - Identify Stakeholders & their Interests ( Symbol : *) {Most Important Part}

      Identify the stakeholders and their interests.

      1. First Stakeholder: is the State, which is represented by the young Civil Servant (the Decision-maker)
      2. Second Stakeholder: Young civil servant against whom the complaint has been logged. (To prove the innocence)
      3. Third Stakeholder: Mafia, the musclemen, corrupt office staff. (to continue illegal mining, and to exploit the tribals).
      4. Fourth Stakeholder: Tribal people

      Identify if there are any conflicts between the interests of the stakeholders.

    • Step 3 - Identify conflicts between Interests of Statements (Symbol : #)

      1. Here it is NOT conflict of Interests. It is general conflict between two peoples interests, it is more of a Conflicting Interests
      2. Ethical Dilemma can be between
        1. Actions
        2. Ideologies

      The interest of the State is to prevent illegal mining

      The interest of the public (tribals) vs. Personal interest (musclemen)

      Identifying the ethical dilemma (value v/s value)

    • Step 4 - Possible Courses of Actions on the basis of Value Conflicts according to Step 3

      1. To identify the course of action
      2. The course of action must be argumentatively presented
      3. That is, it must argue not just the demerits or reasons for not choosing the rejected courses of action, but also identify the possible demerits of the chosen course of action
      4. Once you identify the demerits, then you can work to address them either in the medium term or long term - This Part will get you marks

    • Step 5 - Choose your Supposedly Taken Path

      1. Put the Raionale of your choice
      2. More Importantly recognise the demrits of your choice *
      3. and put in word that you will recognise it later

    • Step 6 - Conclusion

      My actions courage, perseverance, commitment to constitutional values, etc

  • Strcuture of a Case Studies Answer - Summary (For Revision)

    1. Introduction - Tell the examiner that why this question has been asked
    2. Step 1 - Identify the Facts of the case. (Underline)
    3. Step 2 - Identify Stakeholder & their Interests. (* with Number)
    4. Step 3 - Identify Conflicts between Interests (#)
    5. Step 4 - Identify Possible Course of Action on the basis of Interests in Step 3
    6. Step 5 - Choose one Possible Course of Action with a good Rationalise
    7. Step 6 - Recognise & Write the Demerits of your choice
    8. Concusion - Actions, Courage, Perseverance, Commitment to Constitutional Values

        Definition : The process of imaging visualising oneself as someone else and acting like him is explained as role play.

        Example : Making the Most Notorious kid the Monitor of the class himself

        1. There is no objective way of determining what is right or wrong for all time to come
        2. It is neither absolute nor universal and is specific to the context ie ethics is contextual
        3. It operates at different levels - individual, organisational, socio-cultural, political, etc
        4. Ethics are maintained and sustained by a sense of responsibility rather than mere accountability to the external agency
        5. Ethics can transcend the narrow stipulations of laws, it has to be more of a internalised process by a feeling of responsibility
        6. There is no one determinant of ethics, there are multiple determinants

Country Wise Facts

  • India-Oman Relations
    • Sultan Haitham bin Tarik's Visit: First visit to India since assuming power in January 2020.
    • Historic Visits: Prime Minister Narendra Modi's visit in 2018; key agreements on trade, defense, and security.
    • Strategic Location: Oman's geographical proximity and strategic ports.
    • Strategic Partnership: Formalized in 2008; focuses on mutual trust and shared interests.
    • Defence and Security: MoU in 2005; joint exercises with all Indian defense forces.
    • Trade: Bilateral trade reached $12.388 billion in FY2022-23; significant Indian investment in Oman.
    • Indian Diaspora: Approximately seven lakh Indians in Oman.
    • Regional Stability: Oman's neutral stance in regional issues.
    • Duqm Port MoU: Landmark in security cooperation; logistics for Indian naval ships.
    • Energy Cooperation: Significant crude oil exports to India; discussions on joint exploration and connectivity projects.
    • RuPay Card Launch: Represents financial cooperation and digital infrastructure integration.
    • IMEEC and SAGE: Potential convergence on a 1400 km gas pipeline project.
  • India-Israel Relations
    • Historical Stance: India initially opposed the two-state solution in 1947.

    • Current Status: India maintains ambassadors in both Tel Aviv and Ramallah.

    2. PVTG (Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups) Overview:
      • According to the Office of the Registrar General of India (ORGI), the PVTG population is not in decline. This contrasts with the Parliamentary panel's state-wise data indicating a 40% reduction.
      • The Indian government's scheme for PVTGs is named "Pradhan Mantri Adivasi Nyay Maha Abhiyan" (PM JANMAN).
      • There are 75 PVTG communities across 18 states. In Orissa, there are 15, in Andhra Pradesh 12, and one each in Rajasthan, Tripura, and Manipur.
      • According to the 2001 census, all four communities in the Andaman and one in Nicobar are PVTG.
    3. PM JANMAN Scheme:
      • The total corpus for PM JANMAN is ₹24,000 crore.
      • ₹15,000 crore is allocated for infrastructure development, including roads, water pipes, pucca homes, multipurpose halls, and hostels.
      • The scheme also includes Van Dhan Vikas Kendras and off-grid solar systems.
    4. Implementation and Oversight:
      • Multiple ministries and the Scheduled Tribe Component (STC) will implement the scheme.
      • The Parliamentary Standing Committee (PSC) on Social Justice and Empowerment flagged the lack of data in 2013.
      • The National Advisory Council (NAC) suggested that the Ministry of Tribal Affairs (MotA) conduct a census specifically for PVTGs, focusing on various parameters including education and health.
    5. On the recommendation of Dhebar Commission in 1973, primitive tribal groups were made, which was renamed as particularly vulnerable tribal groups in 2006

  • Global Status Report on Road Safety 2023 by WHO

    1. 2/3rd death amongst people of working age
    2. Risk of Death is three times higher in low income than high income countries, where as low income countries have just 1% of worlds motor vehicles
    3. Over All Road Fatality Decreased but India saw 15% Increase in Fatalities
    4. Road Crash Cost Indain Economy 3 to 5 percent of GDP Acc to World Bank
    5. Reason : Traffic Congestion, Poor Road Infra, Vehicle Condition, Paucity of Traffic Police, Weather Condition, Inadequate Emergency Response, Low Awareness
    6. Weak Vehicle Safety Standard
      1. Indian Crash Test at : 56 kmph
      2. New Car Assesment Porgramme : 64 kmph
    7. Inadequate Licensing & Training - in 2018, 26% of all road accdient drivers did not have license
    8. Global Road Safety Report 2018
      1. India 3 and 4 out of 10
      2. Chine 8 & 9 out of 10
      3. Sri Lanka
    9. Paucity of Police : 30% of Positions and 39% of Sanctin was vacant in 2018 by Bureau of Police Research and Development
    10. Work by Govt
      1. Bharat New Car Assesment Program
      2. MVA 2019
        1. Auotmated Fitness Testing
        2. creation of Solatium Fund (for victims of Hit & Run Accidents)
        3. Protection of good samaritan from Civil and Criminal Liability
        4. Creation of National Road Safety Board
      3. Integrated Road Accident Data Base System by MoRTH funded by World Bank
      4. National Road Safety Policy 2010 on reccomendation of S Sundar Committee → Creation of National Road Safety Council
      5. District Road Safety Committee - Estab for each dis under MVA 1988
      6. Vehicle Scraping Policy
      7. Global Agreements
        • Agreement concerning the International Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Road,
        • Adoption of Uniform Conditions for Periodic Technical Inspection of Wheeled Vehicles and Reciprocal Recognition of Such Inspections
        • Agreement concerning the Establishing of Global Technical Regulations for Wheeled Vehicles, Equipment and Parts

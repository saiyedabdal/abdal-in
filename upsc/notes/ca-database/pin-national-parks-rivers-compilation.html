  • Sohagibarwa Wildlife Sanctuary

    Sohagibarwa Wildlife Sanctuary is located in the Maharajganj district of Uttar Pradesh. It was declared as Wildlife Sanctuary in June 1987.  On the northern side, it shares the international boundary with Nepal, and on the eastern border is Bihar’s Valmiki Tiger Reserve. The vegetation of this area is of North Indian Moist Deciduous type. It is drained by the great Gandak, the little Gandak, Pyas and Rohin rivers.

    Context – The Chief minister of Uttar Pradesh directed officials to conduct archaeological excavation in the Sohagibarwa Wildlife Sanctuary.

  32. Adjournemnt Sine Die

    "Adjournment Sine Die" is a term used in legislative bodies to signify that the assembly is adjourned without any specified date to reconvene. It essentially means "without day," indicating that the session has concluded its business and there are no current plans to meet again. This can occur at the end of a legislative session, when all the business is completed, or when the body decides to adjourn indefinitely. The term is Latin, with "sine" meaning "without" and "die" meaning "day." It's a formal way to conclude the proceedings, leaving it open-ended as to when, or if, the assembly will reconvene.

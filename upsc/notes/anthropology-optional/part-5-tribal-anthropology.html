      1. It was an Executive Order
      2. Ordinary Course weren't allowed to give jurisdiction for tribals of this region
      3. Because there was a Big Revolt amongst Tribals against the Hindu Zamindars

      1. Separate Tribunals were created with Tribal Chiefs as their heads
      2. These Tribunals were called Hill Assemblies
      3. Previous Regulation was restricted to Raj Mahal Hill in 1782. Now Demand started by Other Tribes of Chota Nagpur Plateau
      4. British Administration repealed the 1796 Regulation in 1827

      1. It was a result of Kol Uprising of 1830
      2. Now Entire CNP came under special administration → Hence it was now called as a Non Regulated Area
      3. Non regulated Area Meant
        1. There will be No Govt Officials

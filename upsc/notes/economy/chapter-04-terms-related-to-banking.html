  • NPA - Non Performing Asset

    • Mind Map

    • Summary

      1. Standard Asset (SA)

        On this Loan Bank is Getting Regular Interest or the Bank is Able to Recover the Principal Amount

      2. Special Mention Account (SMA)

        1. Default of Loan from 1 Day to 90 Days)

        2. Types of SMA

          SMA 0 → < 30 Days

          SMA 1 → 31 to 60 Days

          SMA 2 → 61 to 90 Days

        3. It is a loan which has not turned into NPA but is showing Incipient Stress

        4. Even Before Loan could turn into NPA, Bank has to take Corrective Actions

      3. Non Performing Assets (NPA)

        1. Default for more than 90 Days
        2. Types of Assets
          1. Sub Standard → > 90 Days && < 1 Year
          2. Doubtful Asset → > 1 Year
          3. Lost Asset → Auditors of Bank and RBI can declare them Lost Asset

  • Special Case of NPA for Agriculture Sector

    1. A Farmer in India can take two types of Loans

      1. Short Term Loans → < 1 Year

        For Crops Which can be Harvested within 1 Year

        Example: Rice, Wheat, Mustard, Millet, Tomato

      2. Long Term Loans → > 1 Year

        For Crops Which can be Harvested within 2/3 Year

        Example: Sugar Cane, Apricots, Horticulture Products, Plantation Crops like Tea Coffee Rubber

    2. Criteria for Loans for Agriculture Sector

      1. Short Term Loans
        1. If There is a Default on 2 or More Cropping Season
        2. Loan will be declared as NPA
      2. Long Term Loans
        1. If there is a Default on one or more cropping season
        2. Loan will be declared as NPA

  • PCR - Provisioning Coverage Ratio

    1. Why do we need Provisioning Coverage Ratio

      1. To Avoid Bank Run → RBI has Mandated Reserve Requirements like CRR and SLR
      2. CRR & SLR → % age of NDTL
      3. PCR → % age of its Profits

    2. How Much Profit Should it set aside. Depends upon

      1. Loan Amount
      2. Categories of Loan
        1. Standard Asset
        2. Sub Standard Asset
        3. Doubtful Asset
        4. Lost Asset

    3. Why is the Bank Asked to do so

      To Withstand the Risk that Might Arrive from NPA’s

    4. A Case on PCR for a NPA

      1. Std Asset PCR is 0.25% to 0.4% of Loan Amount

        Depending on Category of Loan, %age varies

        1. Agri - 0.35
        2. Industry - 0.25

      2. Sub Std Asset PCR is 15% of Loan Amount

      3. Doubtful Asset PCR is 25 to 40% of Loan Amount

      4. Lost Asset PCR is 100% of Loan Amount

    5. Disadvantage to Bank

      1. The Profit of Bank gets Locked Up
      2. This is a Issue for the Bank as it hampers the Cash Flow

  • Philosophy Behind CAR

    1. Banks should have its own Capital to withstand losses arising from NPA’s
    2. Why → Banks are Financial Intermediaries
    3. Hence, they should have their own money in the Bank for more seriousness by the owner
    4. under CAR - Bank has to maintain its own Capital
    5. this Capital Should be adequate enough to combat Risk Arising out of NPA
    6. CAR = (Capital) / (Risk Weighted Assets) 100
    7. At 8% CAR and RWA 100, Capital needed is 8 Rs
    8. At 8% CAR and RWA 200, Capital needed is 16 Rs

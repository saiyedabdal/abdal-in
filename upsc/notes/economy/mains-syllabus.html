    • Basics of MSP

      1. Covers : 22 Commodities
      2. Decisions
        1. Recommendations by CACP : Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices
        2. Announcement by CCEA : Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs
      3. MSP is declared as 1.5 times the Production Cost
      4. Approaches to Measure Production Cost
        1. A2 Approach - Cost of Input Paid by Farmers
        2. A2 + FL Approach - Included All Input Paid + Implied Cost of Family Labour
        3. C2 Approach : All Paid + Unpaid Input
      5. Swaminathan Cmt recommended following of C2 Approach but presently we are following A2 FL Approach

  • Chapter 3 - National Monetisation Pipeline

    • Benefits
      1. Efficient Utilisation of Resources
      2. Investment in Capital Expenditure
      3. Increase GDP
      4. Boost Infra Development
      5. Increase PPP
      6. No Burden on Fiscal Deficit
      7. Risk of Pvt Sector is less as maintenance is done not creation
    • Challenges
      1. Getting PPP is tough - Lack of
      2. Pvt Sector may charge more
      3. Deciding time for Monetisation
      4. Proper Listing of Assets
      5. Red Tapism in the process
      6. Less Transparency
      7. Only Big Business Conglomerates can participate

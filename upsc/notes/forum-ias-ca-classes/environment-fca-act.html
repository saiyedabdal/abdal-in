Environment (FCA Act)

in this question draw maps, draw western ghats

  • News

    Context is that “Van Sanrakshan evam Samvardhan Adhiniyam” VSESA

  • Terms

    • Legal

      Note: forest conservation in the concurrent list

      1. Notified Forest - those forest lands which are recorded in any forest records.

        it is notified under IFA, 1927 and other rules

      2. Reserved Forest has maximum protection - all activities are prohibited unless permitted

      3. Deemed Forest means forest those areas that fits the dictionary meaning of forest or that resemble a forest. they may not be notified but resemble forest

    • Geographical Aspect

      1. Forest Cover : Land Area where the canopy density of more than 10% and an area of 1 hectare of more
        1. Cover > 70% → Very Dense Forest
        2. Cover 40-70 → Moderate Dense Forest
        3. Cover 10 - 40 → Open Forest
        4. Less than 10% → Scrub
      2. tree cover is tree patches of less than 1 hectare outside the reported area

      Note : forest area is legal definition and forest cover is geogrpahical term

  • FCA, 1980

    1. Objectives : Conservation of Forest
    2. focused on key features

      1. state or authorities are restricted from making decisions related to de reservations of forest

        for example diverting forrst land for non forestry purposes for ex quarrying mining or other infrastructural restrictions

      2. any kind of diversion requires prior permission from the central government

      3. it also talks about principles of compensatory afforestation, for any diversion it has to be compensated

      4. if there is any violation, there will be penalities incuding impriosnment

      5. as per environmentalist, this act helped in reudiucng deforestation

  • SC Cases

    1. SC in Godavarman Tirumulpad Case,

      1. SC Expanded the definition of forest to include even deemed forest (an area which first the dictionary meaning of forest)
      2. Each State Govt had to constitute a state expert committee (SEC)to identify such lands that are deemed forest

    2. Implications of this Judgement

      1. led to the complete stoppage of all activities related to forest
      2. Delay in Environmental Forest Clearance
      3. there was a delay wrt to forest clearance
      4. there was a fear that the law would extend to even private plantations
      5. people were in fear that forest land would also go, In India land to population ratio is also very skewed
      6. hindrance in remote area and border states, will lead to left wing extremeis
      7. it also affected any type of afforestation outside forest area, it affectted our, NDC Nationally Determined Contribution, whcih we submitted into paris agrement on UNFCCC. one of the key goals is to increase and create additional carbon sink of 2.5 to 3 billion tons by 2013. in this the most important aspect is increaing the foret cover, hich is imp for bio diersity protection and over all climatic aspects and it is also important for carbon sink or carbon sequestration and it is also imp for carbon sequestration

  • FCA Act, 2023

    1. Objectives: is now Augmentation
    2. Key Features
      1. Forest Area :
        1. Land Notified under forest called notified forest area and
        2. second land not in the First Category but notified as a forest on or after 1980
      2. Exemptions

        1. the act will not be applicable to a land-diverted before 1996

        2. no need of prior approcal for stretagic security or defence projects

          land within 100 km from the border areas in the international border, LOC with pakistana and LAC with China. the permission is exempted

          China is using Xiankong villages is being countered by India by vibrant village program

        3. there is also an exemption to public utility projects in left-wing extremist affected areas it also permits certain activities

        4. permitted activities include setting up a zoo, safari, eco-friendly tourism

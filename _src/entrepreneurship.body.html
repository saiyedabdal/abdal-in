02

Built, then handed off

Inside sales. It began with four of us calling customers from our own phones. About fourteen months later: a team of 85+ with a multi-crore monthly run-rate, and a move from a 35-seater to an 85-seater office. It now runs as its own department.

Retail. Everything except BD and projects was mine for up to 15 stores: store openings, operations, revenue programmes, training and 50+ hires. It now sits with a retail team, and the network has since grown to 29 stores.

Side quest — ORM. Set up the system, shifts, SLAs and first-response times, kept sentiment in check across social and brought in social listening. It showed up in Meta conversions and in how the brand is seen.

Side quest — customer insights. Calling customers and turning what they say into reports. R&D used them to ship v2s, marketing to sharpen ad angles and product pages, logistics to trace returns, sales to stop over-promising, and production to check demand before scaling.

Along the way: got Frido onboarded on Zepto, from first outreach to go-live, and built the decks and review material for investor relations with leadership.

03

What I'm building now

Frido Care. Assessments, physios and the right product, together. An app and partnerships are on the way, along with clinical and consumer trials and new protocols: Ergo Workspace Assessment, Posture 360 and Personalised Footwear.

Frido Care Academy. Training content for physiotherapists, now also growing into a YouTube channel.

Agentic AI. Agents that work across voice and chat. We'd rather own the logic than rent it, so we're building our own control layer and guardrails. They're going live across support, store visits, Frido Care, customer insights and marketing.

Retail OS. Acting as product manager for it and for the wider shift of our tech in-house.

“Building from zero or putting AI agents to work? Always up for comparing notes.”

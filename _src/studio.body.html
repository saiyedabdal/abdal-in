Where it started

Illiterate but experienced

The first thing I ever published — a short essay in the Friday Kidz pages of the Central Chronicle, written at fourteen. It’s about a rag-picker named Anil, and what he taught me about the gap between literacy and experience. The voice is younger; the preoccupation hasn’t really changed.

The written versions of these — and a hundred and sixteen more — are in the poetry archive.

The journey

How it actually went

  1. 2013–15

    DPS Durg

    School

    Head Boy · Pro-Vice Chairman Cup for best all-rounder, from the Chief Minister · inter-school debates in Hindi and English · gold medal in the programming contest · distinction in the Australian National Chemistry Quiz

  2. 2017

    Bhilai

    Founded csvtuadda.com

    A student portal built in my second year that reached 10,000 students. It won the Global Student Entrepreneurs Award — and it taught me what an uncertain revenue stream does to a good idea.

  3. Jan 2018

    UNDP · Bangkok

    Asia-Pacific Youth Exchange

    Led Project SILI — social insight by local immersion — for the community of Bangnampeung, and pitched it at the UN.

    UN Certificate of Honour — best project in Asia & the Pacific

  4. Feb 2018

    Manila

    Project Glasstic

    Built during a local immersion in Bulacan, then pitched to the Vice President of the Philippines and acknowledged at the Asian Development Bank.

  5. Feb 2018

    Dhaka

    EO GSEA, South Asia finals

    Represented Central India at the Entrepreneurs’ Organization finals, and was awarded by the Commerce Minister of Bangladesh.

  6. Mar 2018

    Chennai

    Smart India Hackathon

    Led team ReformTech at the country’s largest hackathon. Our project was taken up by the Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs.

    National second runner-up

  7. 2018–19

    Cameroon

    Business Analyst, Quadrant2E

    A year of consulting work a long way from home — and the first time the job was not a classroom exercise.

  8. Jan 2019

    London

    International Public Speaking

    Selected to represent India at the English-Speaking Union’s international competition in London.

    Represented India

  9. May 2019

    Bhilai

    Founding President, Toastmasters

    Chartered the state’s first community Toastmasters club and led it as founding president. Most of the speaking since traces back here.

  10. 2020–22

    Bhilai

    Student of the Year, then a company

    The Chairman’s Trophy for best all-rounder — and the years I built a healthcare business from nothing, licences upward, working the floor through Covid.

  11. 2022

    Delhi

    The UPSC years begin

    Raus IAS — where a computer-science graduate went and learnt history, polity and anthropology. That chapter has its own page.
